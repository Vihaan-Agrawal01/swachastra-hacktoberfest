Quick guides to make the right waste decision easier.

🟢

Wet / biodegradable waste

Keep food scraps and other suitable organic waste separate. Follow your local collection rules for composting or collection.

Daily habit
🔵

Dry / recyclable waste

Keep clean paper, cardboard, metal and accepted plastics dry. Check local rules and avoid mixing contaminated items with recyclables.

Segregate
⚠️

Hazardous or sanitary waste

Do not put batteries, chemicals, sharps or medical waste into ordinary bins. Use designated local collection and safe handling guidance.

Special handling
📍

Report dumping responsibly

Record the location and context without confronting anyone. Avoid capturing faces, vehicle plates or private details unless essential.

Civic action
🧤

Clean up safely

Use suitable protection and only handle ordinary litter you can safely manage. Leave hazardous or unknown materials to trained teams.

Safety first
🏆

Invite neighbours to segregate consistently this week. Focus on sustained behaviour and verified impact, not simply the number of uploads.
